Quick answers

Good to know

Will polishing damage my paint?

No. We check your paint first and polish gently. If your car needs anything different, we tell you before we start.

How long do you need my car?

About 48 hours, so the coating can harden indoors.

Why is the price “from”?

It depends on your car’s size and condition. We confirm the exact price before any work begins.

What is ceramic coating?

A clear, hard layer that bonds to your paint. It keeps the shine, makes water run off and makes the car much easier to wash.
